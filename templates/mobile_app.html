Notifications

Real-time APNs alerts

Receive ticket, approval, and security-event notifications when Apple push credentials are configured by an administrator and the device grants notification access.

Connect the app

  1. Install a signed build from the ServiceOps iOS repository.
  2. Enter this ServiceOps server's HTTPS address. For local development on a physical iPhone, use the Mac's LAN address rather than 127.0.0.1.
  3. Sign in with your own account and allow notifications when prompted.

Your administrator must configure the APNs team ID, key ID, private key, and bundle identifier wijesundara.com.ServiceOps before remote push delivery can work.
