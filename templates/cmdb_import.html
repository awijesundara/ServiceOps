Inventory source of truth
Identity & hardware

Name, serial, manufacturer/model, management IP, site/location and rack placement become structured CMDB fields.

Classification

Recognized role terms map to controlled CI classes. Custom NetBox roles stay visible as attributes and use the neutral Device class.

Status & lifecycle

Operational status and lifecycle are mapped separately. Offline is Down—not Retired; planned/staged records are Planned.

Virtualization

VM platform is an operating system and cluster is a logical grouping. Both remain attributes; site becomes Location.

View complete synchronization rules

Always refreshed: identity/hardware fields, status, lifecycle, recognized environment custom field, and rack placement. Values removed in NetBox are cleared in ServiceOps.

Preserved as NetBox attributes: tenant, functional role, platform, raw status, tags, comments, custom fields, interfaces, console ports, power ports and inventory items.

Never inferred: NetBox tenant is not an owning support team; role is not accepted verbatim as a CI class; cluster is not a physical location. Operational ownership and business data remain ServiceOps-controlled.

Matching: typed NetBox object ID, then serial number for physical devices, then exact name. Typed IDs prevent a device and VM with the same NetBox numeric ID from overwriting each other.

Import from spreadsheet

Upload a CSV, paste CSV text, or point at a published Google Sheet (File → Share → Publish to web, or a link shared as "Anyone with the link can view"). Recognized columns: Host/Name, Owner/System Owner (resolved to an owning support team, not an individual), Description, Environment, Serial Number, Vendor, Model, Location, Cost Center, Business Criticality, Lifecycle State, Install/Shipped Date, End of Support/Warranty Expiry, IP Address, State/Status (decommissioned rows are detected automatically). Any other column in the sheet (CPUs, RAM, Builder, iDRAC, DNS, …) is kept as-is on the CI's Additional imported fields. Fields on CIs synced from NetBox that NetBox already owns are left untouched.
