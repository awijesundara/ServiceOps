Automation rules

Automation rules watch for events—such as a ticket changing state, an SLA being breached, or a schedule becoming due—and perform predefined, audited actions. They cannot run arbitrary scripts.

How it worksWhen an event occurs, ServiceOps checks each active rule. If its conditions match, ServiceOps performs its controlled actions and records the result below.
