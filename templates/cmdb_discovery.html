CMDB

Agentless discoveryi

Discover devices and their connections without installing anything on them. Devices that answer SNMP get full detail (vendor, interfaces, neighbors); other live devices on the network still show up as a bare entry so nothing is silently missed — most phones, laptops, and consumer routers don't run SNMP, so on a typical network expect far more "liveness-only" hits than SNMP ones. A run never adds anything to the CMDB by itself — you review what was found and choose to add some, add all, or discard them.
